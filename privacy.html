Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Ballistic Calculator app ("the app") collects and how it is used. The app is provided by YOUR NAME ("we", "us"). Contact: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

Using the app without an account

The calculator works without an account. Your settings and the values you enter are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

Information we collect when you sign in

  • Account information from Google or Apple sign-in: your name, email address, profile picture link and a user ID. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only the relay address.
  • Presets you save: gun and bullet data you choose to store (names and ballistic values).
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Pro subscription and its purchase and renewal history. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your card or bank details.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To store your presets and sync them between your devices.
  • To check whether your subscription is active.

We do not show ads, do not use analytics or tracking, and do not sell or rent your data.

Service providers

We use these companies to run the app. They process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): accounts and preset storage. Firebase privacy
  • RevenueCat: verifies purchases and manages subscription status. RevenueCat privacy
  • Google Play and Apple App Store: app distribution and payments.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and presets until you delete your account. When you delete it, your account and all saved presets are removed. Purchase records held by Google, Apple and RevenueCat may be kept as required for accounting and tax law.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account and data at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for corrections, by email.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules allow each user to access only their own presets.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
